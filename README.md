This game is called School Grounds made by Ethan George in Computer Programming.

The goal of the game is to survive for as long as possible and defeat as many mobs as you can. You can utilize powerups to temporarily strengthen yourself. Collecting xp from mobs will allows to choose abilities, a more permanent strength boost. 

Controls:
WASD for movement
Q releases a shockwave around the player provided it isn't on cooldown and you have enough shockwaves left
CLICK shoots a projectile towards the cursor (works better with a mouse)

Powerups:
The larger experience orb gives more xp.
The heart powerup instantly gives you some health.
The lightning bolt powerup gives you a speed boost for a short amount of time.
The boom powerup increases how many shockwaves you have left to use.
The sword powerup increases your damage output.
